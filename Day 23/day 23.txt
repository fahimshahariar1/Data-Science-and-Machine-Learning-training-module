Topics Covered: 
Introduction to Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):
EDA is the process of understanding and summarizing data before analysis.
It involves exploring data distributions, relationships between variables, and identifying patterns.

Importance of EDA:
Helps in making informed decisions about data cleaning and preprocessing.
Provides insights into the underlying data structure.
Facilitates feature engineering and model selection.
